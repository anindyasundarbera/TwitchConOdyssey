# San Diego route — fact and game proposal

**Planning status:** Initial proposal, unreviewed. San Diego is the user-approved game setting. This is a stylized route concept, not a GIS map, street-level route, travel recommendation, or claim about a real Main Stage.

## Proposed sequence

**Little Italy / North Embarcadero → Embarcadero waterfront / Marina → Gaslamp Quarter → San Diego Convention Center → fictional Main Stage.**

The sequence is compressed for a short game run. It does not claim exact street geometry, distances, travel times, or a geographically literal route between the named areas.

| Route anchor | Grounded fact | Game mapping proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Little Italy / North Embarcadero | The City of San Diego describes Little Italy as near Downtown's North Embarcadero waterfront. | Start the courier run in a stylized Little Italy block. The first slice stays within this district. |
| Embarcadero waterfront / Marina | The Convention Center visitor guide places the Embarcadero on the venue's west side and describes bay walks and parks. | Use a waterfront connector and park-like visual palette. “Marina” is a game region label, not a surveyed district boundary. |
| Gaslamp Quarter | The Convention Center visitor guide says Gaslamp Quarter is just outside the venue. | Compress it into a distinct urban segment before the venue arrival. |
| San Diego Convention Center | The visitor guide lists the building at 111 W Harbor Drive, San Diego, CA 92101. It notes two MTS Trolley stops in front. | Use the real venue as the final exterior/arrival landmark. Do not name a real interior stage or imply event access. |
| Main Stage | No real stage location is asserted by this plan. | A fictional delivery goal after the venue landmark. |

## First slice

The proposed first slice is **Little Italy only**, with exactly one fictional blocked demo-kit lane and one proposed clear-lane counter. These are game-design placeholders, not reports of a real local hazard. Movement controls, collision effects, counter values, wipe/recovery behavior, and reward formulas remain open for role critique.

## Source notes

- [San Diego Convention Center — Getting Here & Around](https://www.visitsandiego.com/attendees/getting-here-around), checked 2026-11-01. Supports walkability, Gaslamp's position by the venue, the Embarcadero west of the venue, trolley stops, and the address.
- [City of San Diego — Little Italy](https://www.sandiego.gov/citycouncil/cd3/communities/littleitaly), checked 2026-11-01. Supports Little Italy's relationship to the North Embarcadero waterfront.

The game sequence and visual treatment are inferences from these anchors, not statements made by the sources.
