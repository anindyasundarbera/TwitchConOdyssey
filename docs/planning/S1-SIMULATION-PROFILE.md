## Project design inferences selected for S1

**All numerical gameplay values and formulas below are project design inferences, not externally validated facts.** The deterministic engine remains the sole authority over position, physics, collision, time and settlement. Crowd and model output remains advisory and locally validated. The thesis remains: “The enemy is not chat or AI. The enemy is unstructured scale.”

### D-01 — Player agency (`player_agency`)

- Automatic forward travel across **3 discrete lanes (`0, 1, 2`, lane width `1.0`, center `1`)**. Remappable left/right digital actions, initially arrow keys and gamepad D-pad. One press requests one adjacent-lane transition over **0.5 s** (`10`-tick at 20 Hz) using **linear lateral interpolation**; lateral collision overlap is **`|x - 1.0| < 0.4`**. Edge clamping (`left` from lane `0` or `right` from lane `2` returns **`rejected`**) and in-flight transition lockout (`streamer.steer` received while a transition is in flight returns **`rejected`**, no queueing or overwrite) are enforced (`ISSUE-SIM-01` / `DECISION-04` in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md)); no held repeat. Same-tick opposite actions cancel.
- Auto-forward and streamer steering stay active throughout planning, resolution and reward until terminal state, subject to the pause rule. **Only crowd/counter input locks** at resolution; the earlier broad “input locked” language is explicitly superseded for S1. No streamer counter confirmation or override. No valid crowd result means **abstain**.
- HUD: one progress meter, hazard timer and one plain-language action/consequence preview. Reacting requires no high-velocity chat reading. No added control powers.

### D-02 — Survival (`survival_contract`)

- One localized Little Italy obstacle (`blocked_demo_lane`), counter `clear_lane`. Maximum **1 obstacle per turn**, **3 per match**, fixed center lane, no overlap. Hazard proposal/admission is half-open **[0,15.000 s)** of each planning phase: only one accepted human-origin hazard committed before **t=15.000 s** may create the fixed center-lane warning for the entire **[15,20 s)** interval and one deduplicated bounty increment. Bind the warning, counter and increment to that committed hazard ID. A proposal/model result at or after **t=15.000 s** expires for that turn, with no backdating or rollover. If none is accepted by the cutoff, that turn has no hazard, warning or bounty increment. The warning identifies affected/safe lanes; an uncountered obstacle is active only during the first **3 s** of resolution.
- Collision affects the avatar **once per obstacle**: **20% forward-speed reduction for 2 s**; **0 HP damage**, **0 stun**, **0 steering loss**, **0 displacement**. No stacking, refresh or residual effect. A slowdown ends no later than resolution close. At most **2 s slowdown/turn**, **6 s/match**, equivalent to at most **0.4 s** free-speed travel loss/turn and **1.2 s/match**. Over the **100.0-unit** 5-segment San Diego route (`[0,25), [25,50), [50,75), [75,95), [95,100]`) at nominal free speed **`100/88` units/s** (`88.0 s` nominal completion), even maximum 3 collisions complete at **`89.2 s` < `90.0 s` deadline** (`ISSUE-SIM-02` / `DECISION-04` in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md)). These are analytical bounds from the selected values, not live playtest results. No new obstacle activation before the next turn.
- `clear_lane` removes the obstacle before collision evaluation. Missing quorum follows the same bounded uncountered branch. No HP/death/respawn/checkpoint system.
- **Wipe:** delivery has not occurred strictly before the **90 s active-time deadline**. No automatic retry or state/pool carry. A new explicitly started match resets all state. Zero settlement also applies to build-drop; this adds no new build-drop mechanic.
- INV-02 still forbids permanent control loss, repeated stun-lock, unavoidable death and unbounded cumulative/chained harm. Later reachability fixtures must establish safe avoidance from every admitted state; fixed safe lanes and warnings alone do not prove reachability.

### D-03 — Audience accounting (`audience_fairness`)

- A later live path requires an **authenticated platform subject at account level**, linked across chat/button. Reject unlinked input. This is explicitly **not one-human proof**: multiple accounts, automation and Sybil behavior remain possible. Offline actors are visibly labeled fixtures, never asserted humans. INV-04's human-level aspiration is not an achieved assurance claim.
- Freeze the eligible roster at planning start. Joins/leaves apply next turn; reconnect retains subject identity and spent influence. S1 **K=2**, stable-sort subjects and assign alternately. Squads display groupings; they are not equal electoral blocks.
- Each eligible subject has weight **1**, maximum **1 influence/turn across channels**. First valid authoritative receipt wins atomically; **0 replacements**. Repeated eligible-subject attempts return `duplicate`; transport-notification dedupe remains separate.
- For frozen roster size **N**, `clear_lane` requires **ceil(0.60 × N)** votes and at least **1** actual eligible vote. Exact integer threshold: `(3*N + 4)//5`. Otherwise **abstain**; abstain wins ties and N=0. Bots have weight/quorum/vote/bounty contribution **0**, remain visibly labeled, and never impersonate humans.
- Counter votes retain the existing half-open planning window **[0,20 s)** and must bind to the committed hazard ID; the hazard cutoff does not shorten this window. The general input cutoff remains **t=20 s**.
- This threshold prevents a minority of the frozen account roster selecting the counter. It cannot prevent suppressed participation, multiple-account capture or prove human-level fairness.

### D-04 — Fictional Hype (`reward_contract`)

- Fictional **Hype only**, no money/crypto/payout. Pool starts **0**. Each distinct accepted human-origin hazard committed before planning **t=15.000 s** adds **20**, maximum **1 increment/turn**, cap **60**, multiplier **1**. Counter/avoidance does not remove that increment; harm adds no bonus. Deduplicate by committed hazard ID before incrementing; no committed hazard means no increment.
- Reward phases display outcome/pending pool only and never perform interim payout. Delivery performs exactly **one final settlement**, **50% streamer / 50% eligible audience**, and immediately ends active simulation. Any collision, including one adjudicated at the same pre-deadline logical time as delivery, halves total settlement **once before splitting**; further collisions do not halve it again. Wipe/deadline/build-drop settles **0**.
- Audience eligibility requires at least **1 accepted counter vote in the match**; a valid accepted vote counts even when the aggregate counter misses quorum. Danger creation alone earns no audience allocation. Audience shares are equal **exact rational shares**, with **no rounding redistribution**. If nobody qualifies, withhold the audience half. Withheld Hype is not reassigned or carried.
- Bots/fixtures add and earn **0**. Any fixture illustration of arithmetic is explicitly synthetic and cannot mint participant rewards. Legacy `100+25/voter` formulas remain retired.
- **Residual risk:** accepted safe danger still increases the pool, so cooperative safe-danger farming remains possible. Caps and no harm bonus bound incentives; they do not prove grief resistance.

### D-05 — Clock (`clock_contract`)

- Match deadline **90 s active simulation time**; **20 s planning → 5 s resolution → exactly 5.0 s reward**, maximum **3 turns**, **20 Hz authoritative target**. Presentation may describe reward as approximately 5 s; authoritative duration is exact. With no earlier terminal state, turn 1 is planning **[0,20)**, resolution **[20,25)**, reward **[25,30)**; turn 2 is **[30,50)**, **[50,55)**, **[55,60)**; turn 3 is **[60,80)**, **[80,85)**, **[85,90)**, in active seconds. When turn 3 reward reaches **t=90**, process the deadline boundary; there is no turn 4. Auto-forward and steering continue during reward until terminal state, subject to pause. Route distance is **100.0 units** across the 5 San Diego segments (`[0,25)` Little Italy, `[25,50)` Waterfront/Marina, `[50,75)` Gaslamp Quarter, `[75,95)` San Diego Convention Center, `[95,100]` Fictional Main Stage) at nominal free speed **`100/88` units/s** (`5/88` units/tick at 20 Hz, `88.0 s` nominal completion, `89.2 s` with max 3 collisions < `90.0 s` deadline). Diegetic booth waypoints (`ISSUE-SIM-03` / `DECISION-04` in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md)) trigger at **`35.0%` (`twitch_booth`)**, **`60.0%` (`codex_booth`)**, **`80.0%` (`aws_booth`)**, and **`100.0%` (`fictional_main_stage`)**. No verified live runtime timing or real-world deadline claim.
- One monotonic host clock, **integer nanoseconds** and an authoritative sequence. Replay uses recorded **logical time/sequence**. Planning is half-open: receipt exactly at **t=20 s** expires. Zero post-cutoff grace; client/platform timestamps never backdate admission. Clients receive `applied`, `expired`, `rejected` or `duplicate`.
- At equal logical time, process **deadline first → phase-close → pause request → authoritative sequence-ordered commands → effects/settlement**. A pause at a reached deadline cannot suspend it. Within admitted effects, process **clear_lane removal → collision adjudication → delivery detection → one final settlement**. A same-time pre-deadline collision is recorded before delivery and halves settlement once. Delivery must strictly precede the deadline; delivery exactly at the deadline loses because the deadline is first and wipes for zero settlement.
- Model timeout is **min(dispatch + 2 s, applicable admission close)**: planning **t=15.000 s** for hazard proposals/results, planning **t=20 s** for the general input/counter window. No retry. Results at/after their cutoff expire; late output is discarded without backdating or rollover and never blocks a tick. Schema validity is insufficient: revalidate phase, policy, budget, cooldown, placement, physics and idempotency locally.
- Pause freezes **all simulation clocks and effects**, rejects commands, and invalidates pending model work. Resume resets the scheduling anchor with **no catch-up**. Paused wall time does not count toward active time; no real-world completion guarantee follows.

### Delegated remediation decisions (`delegated_for_s1_remediation_provisional`)

Recorded under the requester's Same-Day Deterministic Remediation Grant in [`docs/planning/DECISIONS.md`](DECISIONS.md) (`DECISION-01..04`):

- **D-06 (`failover_contract`, `DECISION-01`, status `delegated_for_s1_remediation_provisional`):** Stage-operator-confirmed failover (`operator.failover`) is admitted strictly at phase boundaries (`turn_tick in {0, 400, 500}`), rejecting mid-phase takeover (`status="rejected"`). Pending uncommitted queue commands at failover are discarded without rollover while committed `[0,15 s)` hazard/bounty state is preserved at `turn_tick == 400`. Offline fixture commands carry `provenance="fixture"` and `execution_mode="offline_fixture"`. Returning to live mode (`operator.recover`) requires a passing health check and exact match of `(tick, turn, phase, state_hash)`.
- **D-07 (`hotfix_contract`, `DECISION-02`, status `delegated_for_s1_remediation_provisional`):** Codex Booth hotfix manifests (`booth.hotfix`) require trusted key signature (`CODEX_BOOTH_TRUSTED_V1`) plus stage-operator approval (`operator_approved=True`), restricted to the flat 2-key numeric tuning allowlist `{"slowdown_fraction", "slowdown_duration_ticks"}` (`slowdown_fraction` in `[0.05, 0.20]`, `slowdown_duration_ticks` in `[1, 40]`), monotonic `patch_version`, matching `base_plan_revision`, and `expiry_tick >= next_turn_start_tick`. Valid patches stage into `pending_hotfix` and activate strictly at turn boundary (`turn_tick == 0`) with `previous_hotfix_rules` snapshot and atomic boundary rollback (`operator.rollback_hotfix`).
- **D-08 (`submission_and_coverage_honesty`, `DECISION-03`, status `delegated_for_s1_remediation_provisional`):** Separates `simulation_and_plan_ready` (S1 deterministic simulation and candidate plan gate) from `live_submission_ready` (external Twitch/video/audio gate). All booths (`twitch_booth` at `35.0%`, `codex_booth` at `60.0%`, `aws_booth` at `80.0%`) are explicitly labeled as fictional, non-sponsor diegetic metaphors (`INV-08`); audio uses procedural/synthesized opt-in Web Audio only. Unrendered visual/acoustic/live-capture features (`FEAT-14-READABILITY-AND-HUD`, acoustic portion of `FEAT-13-ADAPTIVE-AUDIO`, rendered video of `FEAT-15-PUBLIC-DEMO-TIMELINE`, visual overlay of `FEAT-12-BULLET-TIME-PRESENTATION`) are derived mechanically as `not_observed` in headless simulation while `submission_ready` remains `false` until separate live-submission authorization.
