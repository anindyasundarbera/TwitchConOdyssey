## Recommended demonstration emphasis

The strongest pitch is noisy crowd intent becoming fair, visible cooperation. The collective-risk reward and bounded squads give that thesis gameplay substance. Show an understandable before/after outcome rather than a vendor-logo sequence.

The four judging dimensions map naturally: novelty to coordinated crowd risk/reward; community impact to meaningful low-friction participation; implementation to actual Codex development and reliable behavior; design to readable cause/effect and enjoyable viewing. This is our interpretation of the criteria, not a prediction of scores. [Criteria](https://twitchcon-codex.devpost.com/)

Protect evidence of actual Twitch participation and actual Codex development before optional service expansion. Runtime AI and the Hotfix Booth remain proposed features; this research neither approves removing them nor makes them organizer requirements. [Hackathon evidence](HACKATHON.md)

Maintain four distinct evidence streams: human input, API proposal, engine acceptance/execution, and Codex patch/fixture evidence. A fixture can demonstrate the interaction design; it cannot prove live adapters work.
